## Description

```text
Tidy the wardrobe — one satisfying puzzle at a time.

Clothes Sort is a calm sorting puzzle: tap a shelf to pick up clothes from the open end, then tap another shelf to slide them next to matching items. Match groups of clothes until every shelf is empty. Easy to learn; tricky to master on later levels.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a shelf to lift the clothes at its open end
• Tap another shelf with space to place them next to matching clothes
• Sort until all shelves are clear to finish the level
• Level 1 walks you through the basics
• Undo unlocks as you progress (limited uses on harder levels)

FEATURES
• 200 wardrobe puzzles with a gentle difficulty curve
• Cosy wardrobe look and feel, with wooden shelves and cheerful clothes
• Original classical piano recordings, with volume controls and track selection in Settings
• Progress, settings, and unlocked levels saved on your device
• No account, no ads, no internet needed after install

BUILT WITH CARE
Hello, I'm Sachin. I made Clothes Sort as a relaxing puzzle you can play in short bursts. If something's wrong or you have ideas, I'd love to hear from you.

Questions or feedback? Tap Support on this App Store page to send me an email.

Hope you enjoy the game!
```
