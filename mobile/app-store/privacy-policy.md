# Clothes Sort — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: March 2026*

Sachin Chopra ("I") built Clothes Sort as a free app. This page explains how the app handles information.

## Summary

Clothes Sort does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

## Information we do not collect

Clothes Sort does not require an account. The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. It does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves game progress and settings locally on your device, including:

- Levels completed and levels unlocked
- Selected level on the main menu
- Sound effects volume, music volume, and current music track choice
- Small preferences (for example, whether you have seen the undo tutorial)

This data is stored in on-device storage (such as local storage in the app). It never leaves your device and is not backed up to any server operated for this app. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

## Internet access

Clothes Sort works fully offline. The app does not need an internet connection to play. Music, sound effects, levels, and images are bundled in the app.

If you open an optional link to this privacy page or support page from a future in-app About screen, that uses your browser and is governed by those sites' policies.

## Audio

The app plays optional background music and sound effects from files included in the app. It does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

## Children

Clothes Sort is suitable for a wide audience and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
