Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Sachin Chopra built Clothes Sort as a free app. This page explains how the app handles information.

Summary

Clothes Sort does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Information we do not collect

Clothes Sort does not require an account. The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. It does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app saves game progress and settings locally on your device, including:

  • Levels completed and levels unlocked
  • Selected level on the main menu
  • Sound effects volume, music volume, and current music track choice
  • Small preferences (for example, whether you have seen the undo tutorial)

This data is stored in on-device storage. It never leaves your device and is not backed up to any server operated for this app. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Internet access

Clothes Sort works fully offline. The app does not need an internet connection to play. Music, sound effects, levels, and images are bundled in the app. If you open this page in a browser, that uses your network connection and is governed by your browser and this site’s policies.

Audio

The app plays optional background music and sound effects from files included in the app. It does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

Children

Clothes Sort is suitable for a wide audience and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Support

Clothes Sort is an offline puzzle game. Move matching clothes between wardrobe shelves until every shelf is clear.

How to play

  1. Open the app and tap Play on the main menu.
  2. Tap a shelf to pick up clothes from the open end of that shelf.
  3. Tap another shelf that has space to place them next to matching clothes.
  4. Keep sorting until all shelves are empty to complete the level.
  5. Use the level arrows on the menu to pick earlier or later levels you have unlocked.

Undo: From level 11 onward you can undo recent moves (limited uses on higher levels).
Settings: Adjust sound effects and music volume, mute either one, and change the background music track.
Restart / Back: Use the arrow buttons at the top of the play screen to leave or restart a level.

Does the app need the internet?

No. It works fully offline after installation.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app clears your progress and settings.

How do I reset all progress?

Delete the app and reinstall, or clear the app’s data in device settings. There is no cloud save.

Why is a level locked?

Levels unlock as you complete the previous one. You must finish level N to play level N+1 by default.

The music is quiet / loud

Open Settings from the main menu or the gear icon during play. Use the Music and Sound effects sliders (or mute buttons).

How do I report a bug or suggest an improvement?

Send me an email. I read every message.
